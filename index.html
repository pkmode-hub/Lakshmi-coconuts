01 / ABOUT LAKSHMI COCONUTS

Good coconuts
start at the source.

Lakshmi Coconuts is building a reliable supply of whole mature coconuts for homes, retailers and bulk buyers across Hyderabad and Telangana.

We source directly from farmers and offer three product forms to suit different handling and preparation needs. Product grading, packaging and delivery arrangements can be aligned with each buyer’s specifications.
